# Constant provenance

This file lists every constant that affects the submitted file and records where each one came from.

- "External grid" means the value was chosen from a pre-defined grid. Each grid was selected on the external development matches: DFL WPY plus four SkillCorner matches. D12 and D15 also scored their grids on eight PFF development matches, as a veto only. From D47 on (round 12), the PFF development mean was one of the eight groups of the development score that chose the stack's changes and its ownership cost, and a gate of its own (D48, D49, D52); no value was fitted on PFF data, and the build reads none. The search records are in the `development.json` files and the round 12 recipe folders.
- "Hand-set" means a person chose the value when writing the code. It was never searched or tuned.
- No value below was fitted, tuned, calibrated or selected using the public 5-minute ground truth, the provided video, or any human-produced data about the provided game.

## What the build loads

The submission build loads two sets of the same three model groups, and averages their outputs with equal weight:

- the extended-context XGBoost heads (`long_models/`, `retrain_models/long_models/`)
- the first networks (`stack_models/<offset>/current/temporal_models/`, `stack_models/<offset>/augmented/temporal_models/`; D48, player-set networks, three per slot, for offsets 0, 1000 and 2000)
- the seed-97 networks (`stack_models/<offset>/current/seed_models/`, `stack_models/<offset>/augmented/seed_models/`; D48, three per slot)

Since D48 the networks are the stack's twelve player-set networks (two sets × two slots × three seeds; each slot's output is the mean of its three) and the ownership cost is 14 (`stack_trial build`). Before it reads the competition tracking, the build checks the networks, the trees, its code and the switches against `stack_recipe/confirmation.json`. D43's networks (`player_models/`) and cost 10 remain the first fallback (`player_trial build`, `a56574fe…`); D15's networks (`scale_models/w96_s5400/`) and cost 8 are still built by `kickoff build` (`0ddbb342…`).

The build still loads D3's frozen recipes and checks every digest they name (`competition_adaptation.reference`), then replaces each network with the stack's three networks for that slot. The weights of D3's networks (`temporal_models/`, `seed_models/` and their `retrain_models/` counterparts) no longer enter the submission.

The DFL and SkillCorner reference heads in `models/`, `robust_models/`, `skillcorner_models/` and `fusion_recipe/` are never loaded at inference. They are read only when you re-run the context-stage tune or confirm audit, through `external_benchmark.reference`. The context stage itself selected `owner_mix = dead_mix = 1`, which gives the reference heads zero weight.

**Disclosed lineage of the reference-head settings.** The XGBoost settings in `experiments/dfl_transfer_teacher.PARAMS` match an earlier model of ours that was trained on the public 5-minute window, before the organisers' 3 November clarification. Only heads the build never loads use them: the DFL-only, SkillCorner and fusion reference heads (through `train.py`, `robust.py` and `skillcorner_trial.py`) and the D5 students, which were fitted with the robust-head settings. The submitted heads use `long_context.TREE_PARAMS`, which were hand-set on Nov 10 and never searched.

The context stage's island-suppression settings (`island_frames`, `island_confidence` in `context_trial.py`) are not on the joint-decoder path that produces the submission, so they are not listed below.

## Notes

- **Pitch size.** The submission now uses the metadata's 105 × 68 m. Only the two earlier fallback builds still use the 105.3 × 68.0 m from `games.py`.
- **Ball-dropout rate.** The original model set was trained with 60% ball visibility in the masked view, which matches the "41% missing" figure DATA.md publishes. The retrained set draws visibility from 30–90% instead. Both sets contribute equally to the submission. On 11 November we asked the organisers whether a dropout rate may be set from that published figure. Their reply of 12 November gave no yes or no; it restated the clarification and added that anything not explicitly allowed is not permitted. The constant is kept and disclosed (DECISIONS.md, note of 12 November).
- **Holdout reuse.** The external confirmation matches (DFL WQQ and WR9, four SkillCorner matches) were used to confirm several successive stages. Treat them as development evidence. D48's confirmation also scored new long hidden-ball views of WQQ and WR9 and spent the untouched PFF set, and D54 scored the four 2019–20 SkillCorner confirmation matches. The 24 PFF games of D7's training slots were never parsed. Round 13 named them set D (sixteen games) and set E (eight); neither was parsed or used.
- **The direction of attack is read from the provided tracking.** One sign per half, computed at build time from the half's qualifying frames from its tagged kick-off on (D50, "Inference inputs" above). It has no fitted value and reads no label. The technical document (section 7) discloses it as a reading of the input, not learning on the provided tracking, and discloses that the rule was aligned to the kick-off after the first build refused.
